# Privacy & Online Services

yLoad is designed with strict user privacy in mind. By default, no personal telemetry, analytics, or browsing histories are collected or transmitted.

### Search Suggestions
Search suggestions (optional, disabled by default) directly query your configured search engine provider (such as Google or DuckDuckGo) without intermediary proxying.

### News & External Feeds
Any external feed features are strictly optional and can be toggled in settings. No identifiable data or IP addresses are retained.
