yLoad is an ultra-fast, lightweight Android browser featuring a minimalist Via Browser-inspired interface, built-in uBlock Origin adblocker, automatic dark mode, and complete privacy by default.

Features:
- Minimalist & lightweight (<11 MB)
- Built-in uBlock Origin ad & tracker blocking
- Ergonomic Via-style bottom sheet navigation
- Automatic Dark Mode matching system theme
- No telemetry, no background tracking
- Fully customizable search engines & shortcuts
